Make a song in 4 steps

  1. Pick an instrument in the top row. Its sounds appear in the list on the left.
  2. Press ▶ to hear a sound, then + Add. It is placed at the orange line in your song. Sounds stacked one above another play together.
  3. Arrange: drag a block to move it, pull its right edge to make it longer. Click a block to change its volume, variation or fade.
  4. Press ▶ Play. Click anywhere on the time ruler (or drag the slider) to play from that point. Pause keeps your place. When you like it, press Download MP3.
Shortcuts: Space play/pause · Home back to start · ←/→ move one bar · Delete remove the selected block · Ctrl+Z undo.
